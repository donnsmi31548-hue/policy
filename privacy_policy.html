PackLite — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-27

1. Overview

PackLite (“the App”) is a fully offline parking-lot management application. This Privacy Policy describes what information the App handles and how it is protected.

2. Data Collection

We do not collect personal data.

The App does not require an account, does not use analytics services, and does not transmit any data to remote servers. All information you enter (vehicle plate numbers, ticket IDs, timestamps, fee settings) is stored only on your device inside a local SQLite database.

3. Data You Enter

  • Vehicle plate numbers (entered by the operator)
  • Vehicle type (motorbike / car)
  • Check-in and check-out timestamps
  • Computed parking fees
  • Pricing settings you configure

These records never leave your device unless you explicitly export them via the built-in CSV export feature and share them yourself (e.g. by email or messaging app).

4. Permissions

  • Storage / file sharing — used only when you tap “Export CSV” to save or share a report file. No other storage access is performed.

The App requests no location, camera, contacts, or network permissions.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is a business utility and is not directed to children under 13. Because no personal data is collected, no data from children is processed.

6. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

7. Data Security

Your data lives in the app’s private sandbox on your device and is protected by the operating system’s standard app isolation. Uninstalling the App removes all data.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected by a new “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
